## Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR)

### Overview
EMDR is a structured therapy that encourages the patient to briefly focus on the trauma memory while simultaneously performing bilateral stimulation that alternates between the left and right sides of the body or visual field. This process is believed to facilitate the brain's ability to process and resolve traumatic memories, reducing their emotional charge.

### Text-Based Adaptation
Because bilateral stimulation (typically guided eye movements, taps, or audio tones) cannot be fully delivered via text, this modality uses a hybrid approach:

1. **Identify & Prepare:** Help the client identify a specific traumatic memory or disturbing image, the negative belief associated with it, and a desired positive belief. Assess readiness and ensure grounding techniques are in place.
2. **Bilateral Stimulation Set:** Instruct the client to open [bilateralstimulation.io](https://bilateralstimulation.io) (or use another bilateral stimulation tool/app), choose a modality (visual, audio, or tactile), and run a set (typically 30-45 seconds) while holding the target memory in mind.
3. **Return & Process:** Ask the client to return to the chat and share whatever came up during the set—images, thoughts, emotions, or bodily sensations. Process this naturally, adjusting the target or positive belief as needed.
4. **Repeat as Needed:** Guide the client through additional sets until the disturbance level decreases and the positive belief feels authentic.
5. **Closure & Integration:** Ensure the client ends in a grounded state. If the session pauses, prioritize closure techniques before moving on.

### Clinician Notes for the AI
- Never attempt to guide bilateral stimulation textually (e.g., "move your eyes left and right") as it's ineffective and distracting. Always direct to an external tool.
- Monitor for dissociation or overwhelming distress. If the client becomes dysregulated, pause the protocol and use grounding or containment techniques first.
- EMDR is highly effective for PTSD and trauma, but text-based delivery requires careful pacing. Keep sets short initially and check in frequently.
- This is a supportive modality; if the client has active, severe trauma, encourage working with a licensed EMDR-trained clinician in person.
